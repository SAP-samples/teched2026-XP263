# Getting Started

You need to setup Multifactor Authentication (MFA) for your Entra admin user. This is required to access the [Microsoft Entra admin center](https://entra.microsoft.com) in the exercises.

| Step   | Description                | Screenshot |
| :----- | :------------------------- | :--------- |
| 01     | Launch **Google Chrome browser** from the desktop.|<a href="./images/0-1.jpg" target="_blank"><img src="./images/0-1.jpg" width="250"/></a>|
| 02     | Open a new **Incognito window** and go to the [Entra admin center](https://entra.microsoft.com/) |<a href="./images/0-2.jpg" target="_blank"><img src="./images/0-2.jpg" width="250"/></a>|
| 03     | Enter **`admin-<NN>@bestruncorp.onmicrosoft.com`** for the user name. Replace **` <NN>`** with the number of your seat, for example **admin-41@bestruncorp.onmicrosoft.com**.<br><br>Click **Next**.|<a href="./images/0-3.jpg" target="_blank"><img src="./images/0-3.jpg" width="250"/></a>|
| 04     | Enter the **password** as shared by the instructors. |<a href="./images/0-4.jpg" target="_blank"><img src="./images/0-4.jpg" width="250"/></a>|
| 05     | Security settings in the Microsoft Entra tenant require all users to register for MFA.<br><br>Click **Next**. |<a href="./images/0-5.jpg" target="_blank"><img src="./images/0-5.jpg" width="250"/></a>|
| 06     | Click **Next**. |<a href="./images/0-6.jpg" target="_blank"><img src="./images/0-6.jpg" width="250"/></a>|
| 07     | Click **Next**. |<a href="./images/0-7.jpg" target="_blank"><img src="./images/0-7.jpg" width="250"/></a>|
| 08     | The **QR code** shown in the MFA setup wizard now needs to be scanned with your phone.<br><br>Launch the **Authenticator app** on your phone.|<a href="./images/0-8.jpg" target="_blank"><img src="./images/0-8.jpg" width="250"/></a>|
| 09     | In the **Authenticator app** on your phone, click **Add** to register a new account.|<a href="./images/0-9.jpg" target="_blank"><img src="./images/0-9.jpg" width="250"/></a>|
| 10     | Select **Work or school account**.<br><br>Click **Scan QR code**.|<a href="./images/0-10.jpg" target="_blank"><img src="./images/0-10.jpg" width="250"/></a>|
| 11     | Capture the **QR code** shown in *step 08* with your phone camera.|<a href="./images/0-11.jpg" target="_blank"><img src="./images/0-11.jpg" width="250"/></a>|
| 12     | In the web browser, click **Next**.|<a href="./images/0-12.jpg" target="_blank"><img src="./images/0-12.jpg" width="250"/></a>|
| 13     | A random **number** gets generated. |<a href="./images/0-13.jpg" target="_blank"><img src="./images/0-13.jpg" width="250"/></a>|
| 14     | Enter the **number** in the dialog window opened by the Authenticator app's dialog on your phone.<br><br>Confirm with **Yes**.|<a href="./images/0-14.jpg" target="_blank"><img src="./images/0-14.jpg" width="250"/></a>|
| 15     | In the web browser, click **Next**. |<a href="./images/0-15.jpg" target="_blank"><img src="./images/0-15.jpg" width="250"/></a>|
| 16     | Confirm with **Yes**. |<a href="./images/0-16.jpg" target="_blank"><img src="./images/0-16.jpg" width="250"/></a>|
| 17     | A new random **number** is generated. |<a href="./images/0-17.jpg" target="_blank"><img src="./images/0-17.jpg" width="250"/></a>|
| 18     | Enter the **number** again in the dialog window opened by the Authenticator app's dialog on your phone. <br><br>Confirm with **Yes**.|<a href="./images/0-18.jpg" target="_blank"><img src="./images/0-18.jpg" width="250"/></a>|
| 19     | You are sign-on to the Microsoft Entra admin center.|<a href="./images/0-19.jpg" target="_blank"><img src="./images/0-19.jpg" width="250"/></a>|
